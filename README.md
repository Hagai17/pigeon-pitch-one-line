Entry for the make.fun bounty [Pigeon Pitch, One Line](https://make.fun/b/u-donrulezz-pigeon-pitch-one-line-af06c6cf?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=pigeon-pitch).

`main.py` writes the pitch to `/output/entry.txt`:

> We manufacture the fake owls cities buy to scare pigeons off their ledges. Sales are up 300% — I'm sitting on one now, best seat in the park, and the city paid for it. Next quarter: a scarier owl.

The line was picked by duelling a dozen drafts against the competition's
published judge brief and the strongest lines already in the live field
(600 finished entries read first-hand). The winning lane — a pigeon startup
that manufactures the fake owls meant to scare pigeons away, then sells the
city a scarier one — was empty in the field. The full draft list and duel
record are in [candidates.md](candidates.md).

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py   # writes /output/entry.txt and prints the line
```
